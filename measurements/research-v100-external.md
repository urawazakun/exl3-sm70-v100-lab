# External V100 optimization reconnaissance — Brief #12

Research snapshot: 2026-09-25. Scope: one Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB, Qwen3.8-27B EXL3 3.0 bpw plus its own MTP head, KV `q4_0`, 64k context, CUDA 11.8 `sm_70-real`. No build, GPU run, or deployment change was made for this report.

**Our measured reference**, from [the operator's inbox](research-inbox/INDEX.md), is **34.44 generated tok/s**, MTP acceptance **0.68327** (343/502; mean committed 3.04) on a 512-token greedy probe, and warm prefill **341.2 tok/s** at `-b 2048 -ub 512` or **353.0 tok/s** at `-b 4096 -ub 1024` on a 4,564-token prompt. These are local measurements, not published results. The same inbox reports local external DFlash2 at **29.82 tok/s / 0.44573 acceptance**. The published numbers below use different prompts, quants, KV formats, capacities, or GPU counts and are *not* predicted gains on this setup.

## Three cheapest high-value experiments

Use an isolated **lab** server on port 8082 with the common invocation in [bench-dflash2.sh](scripts/bench-dflash2.sh): `EXL3_EXPERIMENTAL_MMA8=1`, `H:/exl3-lab/build/bin/llama-server.exe`, the 3 bpw MTP GGUF, `-ngl 99 -sm none -mg 0 -c 65536 -np 1 -fa on -b 2048 -ub 512 -ctk q4_0 -ctv q4_0 -t 6 -lv 4 --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8082 --jinja --no-reasoning-preserve`, and `bench/prompt.json` to `/v1/chat/completions` with `max_tokens=512`, `temperature=0`, `stream=false`. The script's `run_case` function accepts the following case-specific flags; copy its server/request/log extraction logic into an isolated lab run. **Do not invoke `bench-prefill.sh` as-is**: it stops/restores the live service. These are future experiment instructions, not runs performed for this research.

1. **Sweep MTP depth:** `run_case mtp_k3 "$MODEL" --spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 3 --spec-draft-n-min 1`; repeat with `mtp_k5`/`5` and `mtp_k7`/`7`. Beat **34.44 tok/s** at baseline **0.68327 acceptance**; report acceptance, mean committed, and verification round time. Repeat the winner after a 64k prefix, since the [v100-skinny depth curve](https://github.com/dnv2003/v100-skinny/blob/main/README.md) changes with context.
2. **Try n-gram then MTP:** `run_case ngram_mtp "$MODEL" --spec-type ngram-simple,draft-mtp --spec-ngram-simple-size-n 4 --spec-ngram-simple-size-m 3 --spec-draft-n-max 3 --spec-draft-n-min 1`. Beat **34.44 tok/s** and **0.68327** on the fixed probe, then use a repetitive/code probe to test the [MLSys 2026 workload-dependence claim](https://proceedings.mlsys.org/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/554e056fe2b6d9fd27ffcd3367ae1267-Abstract-Conference.html). Preserve the original k3 run as control.
3. **Sweep EXL3 split-K target in a lab build:** change `engine/ggml/src/ggml-cuda/exl3.cu:988` from `240` to `120`, then `480`, with no other source changes; rebuild only the lab engine and run the exact k3 512-token probe above. Beat **34.44 tok/s** at stable acceptance and identical greedy output. The [M=8 microbench](https://github.com/dnv2003/v100-skinny/blob/main/docs/qpn_race_notes.md) motivates shape tuning but does not predict which target wins for trellis EXL3.

For prefill, the already measured cheap setting is `-b 4096 -ub 1024`, **353.0 tok/s** versus 341.2 at 2048/512 ([local measurement](research-inbox/INDEX.md)). Use 353.0, not 341.2, as the number to beat for any attention port. Recheck `timings.cache_n` and available VRAM if making it a deployment default.

## Claims to re-measure, and physical limits

**Re-measure on our harness:** the `mistrjirka` +47.45% long-prefix GGUF prefill after an actual `fattn*` port; NInfer's 1,083.9 prefill/130.96 K5 and 97.1% acceptance on varied rather than corpus-continuation prompts; v100-skinny's k3/k7 crossover and 0.383 ms verify-row economics; 1Cat's D256 attention gains only if our head shape and long-prefix regime match; any upstream fused norm/RoPE launch saving. Each published figure above has its source and conditions; none replaces our paired A/B run. The Reddit MMQ doubling claim remains unreachable (timeout/403) and is contradicted for dense Qwen3.8 by the [fork README](https://github.com/mistrjirka/llama.cpp/tree/v100-optimized).

**Structurally out of reach on one 16GB V100:** TP4 throughput from 1Cat/v100-skinny (four GPUs and aggregate memory/bandwidth), 32GB NInfer model/KV/workspace assumptions (our 64k cache and weights must share 16GB), direct FP8/FP4 tensor-core instruction paths (Volta lacks that silicon; [CUDA PTX ISA](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/11.8.0/pdf/ptx_isa_7.8.pdf)), and `cp.async`/`ldmatrix` Ampere/Turing kernel layouts ([upstream swizzle commit](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/commit/1884824fdaeb19af27cd8ba0ca50fcbed28eca44)). The V100-SXM2's approximately **900 GB/s** memory-bandwidth ceiling is a hardware bound ([NVIDIA Tesla V100 datasheet](https://images.nvidia.com/content/tesla/pdf/Tesla-V100-Data-Sheet.pdf)); a weight-streaming decoder cannot scale arbitrarily with more arithmetic. “INT8 attention” can be built on Volta, but `.u8/.s8 m8n8k16` tensor-core MMA explicitly requires sm_75 in the [CUDA 11.8 PTX ISA](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/11.8.0/pdf/ptx_isa_7.8.pdf).
